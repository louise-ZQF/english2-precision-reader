真题例句（含翻译）

In 2021, Flo Health faced a Federal Trade Commission（FTC） investigation.

2021年，Flo Health面临美国联邦贸易委员会（FTC）的调查。

The FTC alleged in a complaint that "despite express privacy claims, the company took control of users' sensitivity fertility data and shared it with third parties.

美国联邦贸易委员会在一份投诉中指控称，尽管公司明确声明了隐私政策，但该公司仍掌控了用户的敏感生育数据，并将其与第三方共享。

Flo Health and the FTC settled the matter with a Consent Order requiring the company to get app users' express affirmative consent before sharing their health information as well as to instruct the third parties to delete the data they had obtained.

Flo Health与联邦贸易委员会达成和解协议，要求该公司在分享用户健康信息前必须获得用户的明确肯定同意，并要求第三方删除其已获取的数据。

Section 5 of the FTC Act empowers the FTC to initiate enforcement action against unfair or deceptive acts, meaning the FTC can only act after the fact if a company's privacy practices are misleading or cause unjustified consumer harm.

《FTC法案》第5条赋予FTC对不公平或欺骗行为采取执法行动的权力，这意味着如果一家公司的隐私做法具有误导性或导致消费者受到不合理的伤害，FTC只能在事后再采取行动。

While the FTC is doing what it can to ensure apps are keeping their promises to consumers around the handling of their sensitive health information, the rate at which these health apps are hitting the market demonstrates just how immense of a challenge this is.

尽管FTC正在尽其所能确保这些应用程序在处理敏感健康信息时兑现对消费者的承诺，但这些健康应用程序进入市场的速度表明，这是一项多么巨大的挑战。
